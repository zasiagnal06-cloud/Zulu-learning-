Before you start

Zulu numbers past five borrow structure from the words before them, so once you know one through five and ten, the rest fall into place quickly.

A note on counting people or objects: in full grammatical Zulu, numbers change form slightly depending on what's being counted. What's below is the standalone form you'll hear most in everyday conversation and market bargaining.
