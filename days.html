Before you start

Most isiZulu day names carry the prefix u-, which marks them as proper names — much like how English capitalises "Monday."

A note on the week: in everyday speech, especially in cities, some younger speakers borrow the English day names casually. The isiZulu forms below are the traditional, correct forms you'll hear in formal and rural contexts alike.

Talking about time

Pair these with any day name to place things on the calendar.

Namuhla nah-MOO-shlah
Today.
Kusasa koo-SAH-sah
Tomorrow.
Izolo ee-ZOH-loh
Yesterday.
